Our Story

Two Decades of Patient-First Dental Care

Sri Sai Dental Clinic opened its doors in Yelahanka in 2003, at a time when the neighbourhood was still finding its shape. More than twenty years on, the clinic has grown alongside the community it serves — with many patients returning for years, and bringing their families along with them.

What comes through again and again in patient feedback is not a single big claim, but a pattern of small, consistent things: doctors who take the time to explain a diagnosis before recommending treatment, a clinic that stays clean and organised, and a team that treats anxious first-time patients with the same patience as long-time regulars. That consistency, built visit after visit since 2003, is what "You are safe in our hands" is meant to capture.

The clinic is rated 4.1★ across 91 Google reviews, with patients frequently highlighting clear communication, gentle handling of procedures like root canals and wisdom tooth extractions, and a genuinely welcoming atmosphere for children and elderly family members alike.
